The Lead

Billy Liucci has covered Texas A&M for 25 years. He has never seen an offense this bad.

TexAgs' Billy Liucci traced the slide back to the Arizona State game. Since then, he said, nothing has looked right: not a unit, not a phase, not the quarterback, "and certainly not ... the play calling."

"There just feels to be no design to it," he said.

He still calls the defense "a good defense." It will need to be great tonight.

No. 3 Texas (9-2, 6-2 SEC) visits Kyle Field at 7:30 p.m. ET on ABC as an 11.5-point favorite. A Texas win plus an Ole Miss win over Mississippi State at noon sends the Longhorns to Atlanta before Alabama and Tennessee kick off Saturday.

The Aggies (4-7, 1-7 SEC) play for pride on Senior Day. Liucci set the bar anyway: "I think it's got to be a win ... a close loss is just a loss."

An upset, he said, "doesn't make the season a success, but it can calm down a lot of the anger and frustration."

No. 3 Texas (9-2, 6-2 SEC) at Texas A&M (4-7, 1-7 SEC)

Line: Texas -11.5 · Total: 40.5
Kickoff: 7:30 p.m. ET · TV: ABC

What's at stake for Texas: The Longhorns are the most likely SEC team to face Georgia in Atlanta. Playing in the final SEC Championship Game would be a nice waypoint for Texas in its third SEC season. Finishing 10-2 after all the drama, injuries and sometimes-ugly play would be something to brag about.

What's at stake for Texas A&M: Nothing. The sooner this season ends, the better. The Aggies need to hit the reset button at offensive coordinator, QB, offensive line and at the skill positions. Aggies fans want to fast-forward in a bad way.

Key injuries: - Texas DE Colin Simmons and RT Andre Cojoe remain questionable. RB Hollywood Smothers, DB Kobe Black, OT Trevor Goosby, TE Nick Townsend and DL Hero Kanu are out. DE Richard Wesley moved up to probable Thursday. - Texas A&M ruled out S Marcus Ratcliffe, DT Landon Rink and WRs Terry Bussey, TK Norman and Ashton Bethel-Roman. S Tylan Wilson went to out Thursday. DE Marco Jones is questionable.

My take

🤘 If Texas wins tonight and misses Atlanta, should it rank ahead of the SEC title game loser?

Texas ranks ahead of Tennessee, which it beat in Knoxville, and Bama, thanks to a better collection of wins.

I think the Longhorns should rank ahead of the Vols and Tide barring a blowout loss to Georgia in Atlanta or a Tennessee or Bama upset over Georgia.

Watchability: 🥓🥓 (2 of 5 bacon strips)

🔒💰 Wake Up SEC Mortal Locks

Record: 2-1

  • Florida -5.5 at Florida State
  • Texas -10 at Texas A&M
  • Ole Miss -17.5 vs. Mississippi State · Top pick

These are our locked numbers. Friday morning consensus: Florida -6.5, Texas -11.5, Ole Miss -17.5.

Why the Ole Miss:

It's a chalky day for the SEC. But I'll take Ole Miss to win by more than 17.5 as my favorite bet.

Mississippi State's offense ranks 133rd out of 138 FBS teams in EPA per play since Oct. 3. We're talking 0, 15, 3 and 9 points against SEC teams not named Vanderbilt.

Ole Miss ruled 12 players out for the Egg Bowl. And sure, give the obligatory Rivalry Week caveats about how Mississippi State should play hard. Hate is alive and well in this rivalry in Mississippi regardless of the records.

But I don't see the Rebels faltering here in the final regular-season game (maybe final game, period) of Chambliss' college career.

QB AJ Swann is doubtful. I see no reason for Mississippi State to play him. So the Bulldogs probably struggle to get beyond 14-17 points — and will have to cross their fingers and hope that somehow Parker "Billy" Puckett can generate some explosives. Fluff Bothwell sat out last week and should be healthier as well.

But you're telling me Ole Miss can't score 35 on a team that gave up 41 to Auburn at home two weeks ago?

HOOPS

Two questions

🍊 Is Tennessee the SEC's best team right now, ahead of Florida?

The Vols (7-0) handled Iowa State and play Louisville tonight for a spot in the Players Era 16 title game. Coach Rick Barnes still points to the preseason. No. 2 Duke routed the Vols 103-64 in an Oct. 20 exhibition at Cameron Indoor Stadium.

"I think what happened at Duke was probably the best thing that ever happened to us," Barnes said Thursday. "We just thought we were going to shoot the ball, and we got embarrassed. And after that, we've been on an uptick."

My take

No. I'd still take Florida, Texas and Vanderbilt, in that order, if you gave me a draft right now.

But the Vols are once again No. 1 in effective FG% defense ... after some questioned whether Tennessee looked as stout as it normally does on the block.

Kudos to the 6-foot-8, 248-pound DeWayne Brown for a breakout sophomore season.

🐗 Can Arkansas shoot well enough to be a safe NCAA Tournament team?

The Hogs (4-3) blew a second-half lead to Michigan State. Jordan Smith Jr. had 25 and 11, but the bigs gave him little help. Cooper Bowser and Miikka Muurinen combined for 6 points and 6 rebounds in 39 minutes, per Jeff Goodman.

My take

Arkansas is too talented to miss the NCAA Tournament.

But the resume is light on quality wins so far (0-3 vs. KenPom teams in the Top 130 with losses to No. 8 Virginia, No. 36 Indiana and No. 12 Michigan State).

Coach Cal isn't doing himself any favors to start the season vis-à-vis his reputation as a much better recruiter than in-season coach.

Looking at how his 2025-26 All-World freshmen are doing in the NBA, one could say that team underachieved a bit as well, looking back.

🎙️ HEARD ON THE BEAT

  • Tennessee's QB room: Volquest put a "1% chance" on both George MacIntyre and Faizon Brandon returning in 2027. The reason is the market. A quality portal QB costs "north of six million dollars," the show said. Mississippi State just reset it with Kamario Taylor's reported $7.5 million deal.
  • LSU's portal watch: Locked On LSU's Matt Moscona says Jayce Brown's time at LSU "is done." "It's not an injury thing," he said. Lane Kiffin left the receiver home from Arkansas: "We just made a decision not to bring him." Brown has eight catches this season.
  • Moscona also expects running backs to leave a crowded room. LSU carries six on scholarship. He asked whether Harlem Berry or Caden Durham goes looking for a chance "to be a bell cow" somewhere else.

📝 RECRUITING

Alabama's Thanksgiving commit fills a real need.

Dylan Gill (6-3, 240), the consensus No. 1 JUCO linebacker per 247Sports, On3 and ESPN, committed to Alabama on Thanksgiving morning. He had 69 tackles, 11.5 TFL, 3.5 sacks and 3 INTs at Hutchinson CC and was the KJCCC Defensive Player of the Year.

  • He's Bama's first LB commit in the 2027 class and its fourth commit this week, per On3's Blake Byler.
  • The fit: Gill plays off the ball at inside linebacker. Touchdown Alabama's Justin Smith calls him "light on his feet" and "solid in pass coverage for a bigger linebacker," with three interceptions as proof. He also "flashes power at the point of attack."
  • The path: BamaHammer expects Gill to compete for a spot on the LB depth chart and help on special teams right away. It compares his profile to Justin Jefferson, the JUCO LB who became a standout in Tuscaloosa.
  • The need: Caleb Woodson started Alabama's first 10 games at Mike and went through Senior Day. BamaHammer expects him to enter the NFL Draft, though he can return in 2027. Luke Metz and Xavier Griffin project back.

Also on the trail: South Carolina flipped 4-star RB Tre Segarra from LSU and Missouri flipped 4-star RB Jayden Miles from Florida State. Signing Day is Wednesday.
