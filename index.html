The Lead

Saturday settled one thing and blew up another.

Georgia is going to Atlanta. The No. 1 Bulldogs (10-1) beat No. 21 South Carolina 34-6 and became the first program in SEC history to reach six straight SEC Championship Games, per SEC Network.

Now the fight is for the other spot, and for a first-round bye. No. 3 Alabama, No. 5 Texas and No. 9 Tennessee all won and all sit at 6-2 in league play with one game left.

The moment everybody will remember came in Knoxville. With 3:14 left, Tennessee fans threw golf balls and mustard bottles at Lane Kiffin's LSU sideline, five years after they did it to his Ole Miss team.

And the Big Ten handed the SEC a gift. No. 4 UCLA, No. 8 Indiana and No. 14 Ohio State all lost on the road, per Pete Thamel.

THE TITLE RACE

Who joins Georgia, and who gets a bye

Alabama, Texas and Tennessee are tied at 6-2.

  • Texas goes if it beats A&M and Alabama loses, whether Tennessee wins or loses. Texas owns the Tennessee tiebreaker.
  • Tennessee goes if it beats Vanderbilt and Texas loses, whether Alabama wins or loses. Tennessee owns the Alabama tiebreaker.
  • Alabama goes if it beats Auburn and both Texas and Tennessee lose.
  • Texas and Alabama win, Tennessee loses: It comes down to records against common opponents.
  • All three win: The SEC goes to its fourth tiebreaker, the cumulative SEC winning percentage of each team's conference opponents. Alabama goes only if Mississippi State beats Ole Miss and LSU beats Arkansas. In the other three combinations, Texas goes.

The facts. Texas is the clear favorite for the second spot. Alabama needs help in every scenario.

The CFP picture

The committee's Nov. 17 rankings had Georgia No. 1, Utah No. 2, Alabama No. 3 and UCLA No. 4. The top four get first-round byes.

No. 4 UCLA lost 18-16 in triple overtime at Michigan, No. 8 Indiana lost 27-24 in overtime at Washington and No. 14 Ohio State lost 31-28 to Nebraska. No. 6 Notre Dame and No. 7 Miami both won. No. 2 Utah beat TCU 38-17.

Texas could move past UCLA per AP.

My read: Barring a catastrophe — and even that may not be enough — Georgia, Alabama, Texas and Tennessee are in the College Football Playoff. The next two weeks will determine the ever-important seeding and bracket draw.

The new rankings come out Tuesday night.

MY TAKES

🐯 Kiffin is 6-5 after being favored in all 11. Is Year 1 a disappointment?

LSU has been favored in all 11 games under Kiffin, per Brett McMurphy. Saturday was the Tigers' third straight loss to a Top 10 opponent.

Kiffin is the first LSU coach to lose five SEC games in his first season since Gaynell Tinsley in 1948.

In Knoxville, Sam Leavitt was sacked eight times. LSU ran 34 times for 43 yards and was flagged 16 times, eight of them false starts. Its only TD came after a muffed punt gave the Tigers the ball at the Tennessee 9.

"These guys are a great defense," Kiffin said. "They play really well, really physical."

LSU (6-5) finishes at Arkansas on Saturday at 12:45 p.m. ET on SEC Network.

My take

Of course. Lane's first year in Baton Rouge is a huge disappointment.

In an alternate universe, LSU is in contention for the SEC Championship Game entering rivalry week. But Sam Leavitt and the entire LSU team were the worst I can remember, consistently, in high-leverage situations.

That defense deserved better this season, injuries and all.

It'll be a long offseason for Kiffin. But unless the LSU powers that be take back their infinite money glitch, Kiffin's roster will be deeper and more cohesive next season.

All eyes are on what Kiffin and LSU decide to do at quarterback. If they move on from Leavitt, they'll be fighting with Miami as probably the top-dollar payers in the market.

Your call: Is Kiffin's first year at LSU a disappointment, or is the bill not due until Year 2?

🎟️ Texas or Alabama for a bye?

Alabama was No. 3 and Texas No. 5 in the Nov. 17 rankings. No. 4 UCLA lost Saturday. Texas could move ahead of the Bruins, per AP.

Against Tennessee, Texas won 20-17 and Alabama lost 26-10, per FanSided. Alabama's other loss was 20-17 to Georgia, per Bama Central on SI.

Texas has won five straight and held its last four opponents scoreless in the fourth quarter, per AP.

My take

Texas.

Bama's FBS opponents went 4-6 this week. Their top wins are now Kentucky (7-4), LSU (6-5) and South Carolina (6-5). Those are the only three Power Four opponents with winning records that the Tide have beaten.

Texas wins big game after big game, no matter how many injuries they accrue or how ugly it looks at times. I wouldn't be surprised if the College Football Playoff committee ranks the Longhorns ahead of the Tide this week.

And Texas is the more likely participant in the SEC Championship Game. Win that — as good as Georgia is, Texas isn't drawing dead — and Texas will get a bye.

If they're competitive, I can see the committee ranking Texas ahead of Bama even with a loss in that game.

But based on the results of the last few years, sometimes the best possible position is to get a first-round home game against an inferior opponent instead of entering the quarterfinals rusty.

🏃 ALABAMA WINS ITS FIRST CROSS COUNTRY TITLE

Alabama's women won the NCAA Cross Country Championships in Terre Haute, Indiana, with 120 points, ahead of West Virginia (145) and top-ranked BYU (152), per Alabama Athletics.

It's the women's program's first national title. Caren Kiplagat finished third and Cynthia Jemutai 10th.

Alabama is the first SEC program to win the women's title since Arkansas in 2019, and the first founding SEC member to do it.

The Tide men finished third, matching their best finish (2009). Dismus Lokira was the national runner-up.

"Like I told them afterwards, that was maybe the best race I've ever seen," coach Dan Waters said.
